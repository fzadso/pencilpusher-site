# Your website: how to update it

Hi Mom! Everything on your website can be changed from one page. You don't need to know anything about code. Every change you save shows up on the website **about 1 to 2 minutes later**.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **thepencilpusher.net/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**.
3. The first time, type your GitHub email and password. After that it remembers you.

You'll see two sections on the left: **Artwork** and **Pages & settings**.

**Tip:** In **Artwork**, click the little **grid** button at the top right to see your art as pictures instead of a list. It remembers your choice. Newest pieces are at the top, and the **Pencil**, **Acrylic** and **Sold** buttons narrow the list.

While you're editing a piece, the right half of the screen shows a **preview** of how it will look on the website, and it updates as you type.

---

## Adding a new piece of art

1. Click **Artwork**, then the **New Artwork** button.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: for example *Silver Fox*
   - **Photo**: click **Choose an image**, then **Upload**, and pick the photo from your computer. Any size is fine; the website shrinks it for you. Use a .jpg or .png photo.
   - **Pencil or acrylic?**: this decides which tab it shows under in the Shop.
   - **Size**: for example *16 × 20 in*
   - **Price of the original**: just the number, like *950*. Leave it empty if the original isn't for sale.
   - **Sell prints**: on, unless you don't want prints of this one.
   - **Print price**: starts at 50. Change it any time.
   - The last few boxes (year, a few words about the piece, date added) are optional. You can leave them alone.
3. Click **Publish** at the top, then **Publish now**.

> **Photo tips:** Take the picture straight on, in daylight but out of direct sun, with no glass or frame. Fill the picture with the artwork. Bigger photos look better when people zoom in.

---

## Marking a piece SOLD

When an original sells:

1. Click **Artwork** and click the piece.
2. Switch on **The original is SOLD**.
3. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.

The piece stays on the website with a **Sold** tag. People can still buy prints of it.

---

## Changing a price

1. Click **Artwork** and click the piece.
2. Change **Price of the original** or **Print price**.
3. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.

---

## Removing a piece

Usually it's better to mark it sold, so people can still see it and buy prints. To remove it completely: open the piece, click **Delete entry** at the top right, and confirm.

---

## Choosing the pictures on the home page

1. Click **Pages & settings**, then **Home page gallery**.
2. The list is the row of pictures at the top of your home page, in order. **The first one is the big picture** people see when they arrive.
   - **Change the order:** drag a piece by its ☰ handle.
   - **Add a piece:** click **Add picture** at the bottom and start typing its title.
   - **Take a piece off the home page:** click its **X**. It stays in the Shop.
3. **Also show new pieces automatically:** when this is on, new art you add shows up on the home page by itself, after the ones in your list. Switch it off if you want only the ones you picked.
4. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.

---

## Changing your About text, the Commissions page, or shipping prices

Click **Pages & settings**, then:
- **About me (home page)**: your story, the quick facts, and an optional photo.
- **Commissions page**: the opening sentence, the steps, and the sample pictures.
- **Shop & contact settings**: shipping prices, the email shown on the Contact page, your Instagram or Facebook link.

Make your change, then **Publish** and **Publish now**.

---

## When someone buys something

- **Square emails you** about each sale, the same as any Square payment. The email shows what they bought and whether they chose **shipping** (with their address) or **local pickup**.
- If they picked **local pickup**, email them to set a time.
- If they bought an **original**, remember to mark it **Sold** (see above).

---

## Where messages go

- **Contact form messages and commission requests** are emailed to **Thepencilpusher123@gmail.com**. Just reply to the email to answer them. Photos people attach are linked in the email.
- **Newsletter signups** are saved on Netlify. Ask [your kid] to download the list for you when you want to send an update.

---

## If something looks wrong

- Wait 2 minutes and refresh the page. Changes take a moment.
- Nothing you do in the editor can break the website for good. Every change is saved, and older versions can always be brought back.
- Still stuck? Call [your kid].
